Mnemonics worth keeping

  • Randy Travis Drinks Cold BeerRoots, Trunks, Divisions, Cords, Branches, in order from the neck to the arm.
  • MARMUTerminal branches: Musculocutaneous, Axillary, Radial, Median, Ulnar.
  • LLMLateral cord: Lateral pectoral, Lateral root of median, Musculocutaneous.
  • M4UMedial cord: four Medials (pectoral, cutaneous of arm, cutaneous of forearm, root of median) plus Ulnar.
  • STARPosterior cord: Subscapulars (upper and lower), Thoracodorsal, Axillary, Radial.
  • LOAFMedian nerve in the hand: Lateral 2 lumbricals, Opponens pollicis, Abductor pollicis brevis, Flexor pollicis brevis.
  • C5, 6, 7 raise your wings to heavenLong thoracic nerve roots; injury gives a winged scapula.

Draw it from memory

Once a day: blank page, two-minute timer, draw roots to branches, then check against the map. Start with the big "M" (lateral cord, median, medial cord). Tap a day when you've done it.
